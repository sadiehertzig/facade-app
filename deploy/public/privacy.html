FACADE Photo Shield: Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

FACADE Photo Shield ("FACADE") is a student project built for the 2026 Congressional App Challenge. It explores experimental ways to make photos harder for AI editing tools to misuse. This page explains what happens to your photos and information.

The iPhone app

  • The app processes photos entirely on your iPhone. It does not upload your photos anywhere.
  • It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not collect personal information.
  • A protected photo is saved to your photo library only when you choose to save it.
  • The app may show a link to the team's website. Opening it is optional.

The FACADE website and PhotoGuard demo

  • The website is a private, password-protected research site run by the team.
  • If you choose to process a photo there, it is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the team's private server, hosted by Amazon Web Services.
  • Photos are processed in memory and deleted automatically afterwards. The protection service keeps a job for at most 30 minutes; the demo's temporary files are deleted within about 15 minutes.
  • Photos are never used to train AI models, and are never sold or shared.
  • The server does not keep access logs of your visits.

Testing through TestFlight

If you install FACADE through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports and any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's privacy policy. The team sees only what TestFlight shares with developers.

Limits

FACADE's protection is experimental and is not guaranteed to prevent editing or misuse of your photos.
